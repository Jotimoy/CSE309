# Product Requirement Document (PRD)

## Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define the product requirements for the Smart AI Warehouse Management System.

## Product Vision
To provide a seamless, error-free, and real-time inventory management experience using AI, Computer Vision, and OCR.

## Scope
The system will automate inventory tracking, digitize analog stock information via OCR, and provide real-time dashboards for warehouse managers and staff.

## Target Audience
- Warehouse Managers
- Warehouse Staff
- Business Owners
