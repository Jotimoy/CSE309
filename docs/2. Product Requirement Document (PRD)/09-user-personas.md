# User Personas

## Persona 1: Warehouse Manager (David)
- **Goals**: Needs accurate real-time visibility into inventory levels, minimize shrinkage, and generate business reports.
- **Pain Points**: Manual data entry takes too long, and inventory mismatches cause operational delays.

## Persona 2: Warehouse Staff (Sarah)
- **Goals**: Quickly log incoming and outgoing stock with minimal manual typing.
- **Pain Points**: Scanning or typing barcodes one by one is tedious and prone to human error.

## Persona 3: Business Owner (Michael)
- **Goals**: Maximize ROI, track warehouse efficiency, and ensure customer satisfaction through timely fulfillments.
- **Pain Points**: Lack of high-level analytics and historical trends.
